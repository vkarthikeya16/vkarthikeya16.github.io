A Hidden Story

Words I never knew how to say

Scroll

I don't know where to begin. Some feelings are too heavy for words, and maybe that is why I stayed silent for so long. You have moved on as if nothing happened, while I am still standing in the same place, surrounded by memories of you. My heart hasn't learned how to let go.

✦

"I couldn't confess my feelings for you, so I watched you from afar, being happy with someone else."

I live in a strange dilemma. Some days I want you with all my heart. Other days I convince myself I shouldn't want you at all. Yet no matter how far I try to walk away, something inside me always brings me back to you.

Maybe it is because you are stronger than me. Maybe you have seen more of life than I have. Or maybe it is because somewhere deep inside, I still wish I could sit beside you, rest my head on your lap, and cry without saying a single word.

A moment · Written in silence

I made mistakes. Many of them. Maybe it was my age — 23 and completely lost when it came to matters of the heart. I had never known heartbreak like this before. I didn't know how to speak about love, how to hold it, or how to protect it. Instead, I stayed silent and made you believe I was an asshole. Maybe I am one. (A little sarcasm there.)

But still, I want to thank you. Because you showed me something I had never truly seen before — my flaws, my immaturity, the parts of me that needed to grow. Painful as it is, that truth mattered.

✦

You married someone at 19 to 20 years. Now you understood he is not the right one for you. Maybe I will reach a certain age where I can have the maturity to understand some things in my life.

You will probably forget me easily. I know that. And part of me wishes I could forget you too. But memories don't listen to reason. They come back in the quiet moments. They come back when I close my eyes. They come back when I try to sleep.

"Good or bad, memories never fade; they simply live on as silent whispers in our hearts."

Sometimes I want to cry, but the tears never come. Sometimes I want to be angry, but the anger never stays. All that remains is a quiet guilt that refuses to leave me.

And then there are the moments I can never erase. The day I asked you, "Can I kiss you?" — 9th March 2026, when we were watching With Love — you looked at me and said something I will carry with me for the rest of my life:

"."

No one had ever said something like that to me before. In that moment, you saw something in me that even I didn't understand. To me, love has always meant one simple thing — wanting someone to be happy, even if that happiness doesn't include you.

✦

I still remember the gelato shop — 13th March 2026. Your eyes speak, Pooja. I don't know how many notice that, but I do. I feel your breath — how it changes, the way you see, the way you talk, the way you get emotional and angry — everything I see. I just don't know how to express it.

When I finally said, "I loved you," you looked straight into my eyes and asked, "Now you have the courage to say that?" That moment hit me like lightning. I didn't know what to say. Maybe because you were right. Maybe because I had waited too long.

My way of loving has always been strange. I tease the people I care about the most. That's why I teased you with "andha kanna paathaakaa" and joked about the sandwich at Popeyes with Chi — those little moments meant more to me than I ever showed. You like to have some flirty conversations. You enjoy those silly moments but you never express them.

· · · ✦ · · ·

But somewhere along the way, I crossed a line I should never have crossed. Your diary. Something personal. Something that belonged only to you. Reading it helped me understand you better, but that doesn't justify what I did. It was wrong. And I am truly sorry for touching something that you trusted the world not to see.

Maybe this letter will reach you. Maybe it won't. Maybe we will meet again one day. Or maybe this chapter of my life will end with silence. You might not want to see my face again. I wouldn't blame you for that. But even then, there is one truth I cannot deny —

I loved you. Truly. I always watched you as Sai Pallavi garu from Shyam Singha Roy. And if there was even a one percent chance… I would marry you without hesitation, you stubborn Cancerian girl.

✦

One day you asked me why I take things personally. Why I am nice to people even when I shouldn't be. The truth is, I don't fully know the answer either. But I do know this: loneliness is a terrible place. If I hadn't followed you, I would probably still be there. And I never want anyone else to walk that same empty road.

Yes, my words can be harsh sometimes. But my heart has never wished harm on anyone — especially not you.

"Somewhere in the world, there was an idiot who loved you more honestly than he ever knew how to say."

And I still believe in destiny. If this strange, stubborn destiny ever brings you back into my life again, I will not hesitate. I will tie the knot, no matter what the world says. Let the earth fall. Let the oceans rise. Some promises are stronger than both.

Written with every part of me,
